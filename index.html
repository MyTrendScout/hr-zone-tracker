How it works

A coach in your pocket — from your first long run to race morning.

  • Built around your race date

    Tell us your race date and fitness level. We build a full plan backward from race morning — Foundation, Base, Build, Peak, Taper, Race Week — so every week has a purpose and nothing is wasted.

  • Run the right effort, not just the right miles

    Every workout comes with an HR zone target — not just a distance. Easy days stay truly easy. Hard days push the right systems. Your easy run isn't supposed to feel like a tempo. We'll flag it if it does.

  • Your schedule, not ours

    Pick your long run day — any day of the week. The whole plan flexes around it. Rest always falls the day before, cross-training the day after. No more 5am Saturday alarms in Florida heat because a template said so.

This is a beta

We're not ready for everyone. That's intentional.

MyPaceZone is invite-only right now. We're letting in a small group of real runners so we can build the right thing — not just ship features nobody needs. Access is free. What we ask for in return is honesty.

  • To get access, you need Status
  • A marathon on the calendar This is a marathon training app — not 5K, half, or triathlon (yet) Required
  • A heart rate monitor Chest strap or wrist sensor — something that gives you bpm during a run Required
  • Willingness to give real feedback Tell us what's missing, broken, or just wrong. We'll actually listen. Required
  • Payment Beta is completely free. Full stop. Not required
